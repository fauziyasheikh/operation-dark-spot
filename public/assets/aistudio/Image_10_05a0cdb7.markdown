## Brand & Style

This design system channels the energetic spirit of vintage Sunday comic strips re-engineered through a modern neo-brutalist interface. It balances forensic investigation workflows with playful, tactile, and narrative-driven storytelling. The target audience includes modern researchers, investigative gamers, creative collaborators, and problem solvers who value clarity delivered with wit and tactile punch.

The aesthetic fuses **Tactile Neo-Brutalism** and **Pop-Art Graphic Novel** sensibilities:
- **Thick Outlines:** Bold, unyielding ink-line strokes (3px to 4px) anchor every interactive element, lending an illustrated, physical paper weight.
- **Physicality and Offset:** Hard-edge offset drop shadows replicate physical pop-up stickers, dossiers, and cardstock case files.
- **Warm Nostalgia:** A warm, cream paper canvas balances high-intensity primary inks, preventing digital eye fatigue while reinforcing the feel of physical evidence boards.
- **Micro-Delight:** Asymmetry, comic action burlesques, stamp-style badges, and tactile button presses bring a sense of discovery to standard productivity patterns.

## Colors

The palette references classic comic print registration while adhering to high-contrast legibility requirements.

### Color Logic
- **Canvas Base:** `#FFFDF0` (Warm Manila Paper) serves as the primary canvas background. Elevated dossier cards and internal content blocks shift to crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) or pale parchment (`#FEF9E7`).
- **Primary Ink / Neutral:** `#18181B` (Charcoal Black) is non-negotiable for all structural borders, typography, hard retro shadows, and line art. Never use transparent gray borders.
- **Primary Accent (Case Yellow):** `#FACC15` (Sunny Cheerful Yellow) and `#FDE047` (Highlight Yellow) denote focus, discovery, magnifying glass rings, and primary interactive priorities.
- **Secondary Accent (Classified Red):** `#EF4444` and deep `#DC2626` signal urgent clues, evidence stamps, action triggers, destructive actions, and active sirens.
- **Tertiary Accent (Cobalt Blue):** `#2563EB` and `#3B82F6` serve as secondary cues for blueprints, field notes, and digital telemetry data.
- **Field Mint (Evidence Green):** `#10B981` (minty emerald) is reserved for cleared suspects, verified leads, and successful validations.

Always preserve stark contrast against the cream background. Interactive elements pair filled color backgrounds directly with `#18181B` outlines and label text.

## Typography

The type system blends dynamic, expressive comic titles with clear, readable interface text.

- **Headline & Display Font (`Bricolage Grotesque`):** Delivers chunky, expressive, slightly offbeat grotesque anatomy. Used in ultra-heavy weights (700, 800) for section titles, modal headers, case labels, and speech-bubble callouts. It replicates high-impact title lettering while retaining sharp digital precision.
- **Body & Label Font (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Provides soft, circular geometric curves that blend with the heavy comic framing without sacrificing readability across tables, suspect dossiers, and metadata lists.
- **Styling Directives:** Display titles and badges frequently use slight uppercase transformations, wide tracking (`0.06em` to `0.08em`), or an occasional intentional `-1.5deg` to `+1.5deg` CSS rotation on select decorative sticker badges to reinforce an evidence-board collage aesthetic.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow an asymmetric fluid-column structure inspired by comic panel compositions and detective pegboards.

### Layout Principles
- **Grid Structure:** A 12-column responsive fluid grid on desktop (`> 1024px`) with `2rem` gutters, transforming to an 8-column layout on tablet (`768px - 1023px`), and a 4-column layout on mobile (`< 768px`).
- **Panel Reflow:** Case summaries and dossiers span multi-column cards that pack snugly. Complex evidence boards allow horizontal masonry or pinboard arrangements, collapsing into a linear sequence of stacked, bordered cards on mobile.
- **Rhythm & Padding:** Spacing tokens intentionally lean larger than clinical enterprise dashboards to give thick outlines and hard-edge drop shadows room to breathe without visual collision. Internal container padding uses `space-md` (`1.25rem`) and `space-lg` (`2rem`).

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids soft, diffused, realistic blur shadows. Depth is achieved via **hard retro ink-offset elevation**, creating a tangible 2.5D tactile surface.

### Elevation Levels
- **Layer 0 (Flat Surface / Canvas):** Neutral background panels. Border: `3px solid #18181B`, No shadow.
- **Layer 1 (Standard Card / Resting Component):** Standard dossier panels, interactive tiles, resting buttons.
  - Border: `3px solid #18181B`
  - Shadow: `4px 4px 0px #18181B`
- **Layer 2 (Floating Pin / Active Card):** Highlighted evidence, hovered cards, tooltips, open popovers.
  - Border: `3px solid #18181B`
  - Shadow: `6px 6px 0px #18181B`
  - Subtle upward transform: `translate(-2px, -2px)`
- **Layer 3 (Modals / Urgent Alerts / Dragging):** Top-level evidence files, lightboxes, action menus.
  - Border: `4px solid #18181B`
  - Shadow: `8px 8px 0px #18181B`
- **Tactile Pressed State (Interaction):**
  - Buttons and interactive items invert their elevation when clicked: `translate(4px, 4px)` with shadow reducing to `0px 0px 0px #18181B`. This provides immediate physical click feedback.

## Shapes

The design combines brutalist borders with smooth, playful curves. Containers feel soft and friendly rather than harsh and industrial.

### Geometry Guidelines
- **Core Containers (Dossiers, Cards, Sheets):** Set to `border-radius: 1.25rem` to `1.5rem` (`rounded-lg` to `rounded-xl`). Paired with a `3px` or `4px` outline, this forms a pillowy, comic-bubble perimeter.
- **Buttons and Inputs:** Set to `border-radius: 9999px` (full pill) or `1rem` (chunky squircle) to invite finger or cursor interaction.
- **Badges and Burst Tags:**
  - Pills: `9999px` border radius with solid borders.
  - Comic Action Bursts: Multi-point polygon clipped path (`polygon(...)`) with high-contrast text and a hard drop shadow for "TOP SECRET", "BREAKTHROUGH", or "NEW CLUE" alerts.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Case Yellow):** Background `#FACC15`, text `#18181B`, border `3px solid #18181B`, border-radius `1rem` or `9999px`, shadow `4px 4px 0px #18181B`, typography `label-lg`.
- **Urgent Action (Comic Red):** Background `#EF4444`, text `#FFFFFF`, border `3px solid #18181B`, shadow `4px 4px 0px #18181B`.
- **Secondary (Paper White):** Background `#FFFFFF`, text `#18181B`, border `3px solid #18181B`, shadow `4px 4px 0px #18181B`.
- **Active / Pressed State:** All buttons shift via `transform: translate(4px, 4px)` with shadow dropping to `0px 0px 0px #18181B`.

### Cards & Dossiers
- Background `#FFFFFF` or `#FEF9E7`, border `3px solid #18181B`, border-radius `1.5rem`, shadow `4px 4px 0px #18181B`.
- Internal sections are separated by `2px dashed #18181B` or solid hairline inking.
- Header bars often feature a contrasting strip (e.g., `#FACC15` or `#2563EB`) with a bold monochrome badge and simulated folder tab styling.

### Input Fields & Search
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `3px solid #18181B`, border-radius `1rem`, padding `0.75rem 1.25rem`, typography `body-md`.
- Focus state: Outline remains `#18181B`, shadow snaps to `4px 4px 0px #FACC15`, background stays clean white.
- Search inputs incorporate a chunky magnifying glass icon with a thick circular frame.

### Chips & Evidence Tags
- Compact pills with `border-radius: 9999px`, border `2px solid #18181B`, shadow `2px 2px 0px #18181B`, typography `label-badge`.
- Categorical colors:
  - Suspect: Background `#EF4444`, text `#FFFFFF`
  - Lead: Background `#FACC15`, text `#18181B`
  - Verified: Background `#10B981`, text `#FFFFFF`
  - Classified: Background `#18181B`, text `#FFFFFF`

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size `24px x 24px`, border `3px solid #18181B`, border-radius `6px` (checkbox) or `9999px` (radio).
- Checked State: Background `#FACC15` or `#EF4444` with a thick, hand-drawn-style black checkmark (`stroke-width: 3.5px`). Hard offset shadow `2px 2px 0px #18181B`.

### Specialized Detective Agency Components
- **Case Pinboard Pin:** Floating decorative badge that pins items to the evidence board, sporting a glossy red button head and hard black offset.
- **Top Secret Evidence Tape:** Striped diagonal caution banner (`repeating-linear-gradient` with `#FACC15` and `#18181B`) used as an unreleased section ribbon or locked state banner.
- **Magnifying Loupe Popover:** Contextual card with an oversized circular border and an offset tail pointing to analyzed evidence details.
